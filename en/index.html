FAQ

Everything you might ask

How do I download an Instagram video or Reel?
Send the post or Reels link to @InstaDownloadai on Telegram; the video arrives right there in the best quality (up to 4K) — free, no sign-up. All items of a carousel come together too.
Do downloaded videos have a watermark?
No; the bot never adds a watermark or logo — you get the exact original file from the platform. For TikTok the video arrives without the TikTok logo and without the username watermark — perfect for clean reposts.
How do I find a song whose name I don't know?
Send the video (Instagram, TikTok or Pinterest) to the bot and tap “Find the song”; the bot recognizes it and sends the best quality.
Can I find remixes too?
Yes — just add “remix” next to the song name. The music finder covers Persian, English and any-language remix catalogs.
Can I extract mp3 audio from a video?
Yes — pull the mp3 out of any Instagram, TikTok or Vimeo video. The music finder works too: type the song name — Persian, English or a remix — and get up to 320kbps; if you don't know the name, send the video and let the bot recognize it.
What quality are the downloads?
Videos in the best available quality (up to 4K), songs up to 320kbps, photos at original quality. The bot never downgrades; if the source only has lower quality, you get the best version that exists.
What can the bot download?
Instagram videos, reels, stories and albums, watermark-free TikTok videos, photo and video pins from Pinterest, Vimeo, and music/MP3 via the music finder — all up to 4K and 320kbps.
Is the bot free?
Yes, everything is free (about 80 downloads per day per user) — no card, no subscription. Just open @InstaDownloadai on Telegram and press Start; no login, no phone number and no access to your accounts — files land in your own private chat.
How fast is it? Any file-size limit?
Usually files arrive within seconds; the exact time depends on the file size and your Telegram speed. Each file is delivered up to about 49MB — plenty for Reels and TikToks; if a download stalls, just send the same link again.
Which languages does the bot speak?
Four: English, Persian (Farsi), Arabic and Russian. It asks once on first Start; change later with /lang.

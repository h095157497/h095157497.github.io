FAQ

How do I download an Instagram video or Reel?
Send the post or Reels link to @InstaDownloadai on Telegram; the video arrives right there in the best quality (up to 4K) — free, no sign-up.
How do I find a song whose name I don't know?
Send the video (Instagram, TikTok or Pinterest) to the bot and tap “Find the song”; the bot recognizes it and sends the best quality.
Can I find remixes too?
Yes — just add “remix” next to the song name. The music finder covers Persian, English and any-language remix catalogs.
What quality are the downloads?
Videos in the best available quality (up to 4K), songs up to 320kbps, photos at original quality.
What tools does the bot have?
QR maker (/qr), currency rates (/arz), weather (/hava), translation (/tr) and lyrics (/lyric). The /abzar menu lists them all; QR, rates, weather and lyrics also work right on this site.
Is the bot free?
Yes, everything is free (about 80 downloads per day per user). Just open @InstaDownloadai on Telegram and press Start.
Which languages does the bot speak?
Four: English, Persian (Farsi), Arabic and Russian. It asks once on first Start; change later with /lang.

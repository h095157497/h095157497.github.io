04/FAQCommon questions

Frequently asked.

How do I download an Instagram video or Reel?

Copy the link of the post or Reel from Instagram and send it to the bot @InstaDownloadai on Telegram; the video arrives right in the chat at the highest quality available (up to 4K) and with no watermark. All items of a carousel come together in one go.

Do downloaded videos have a watermark?

No; the bot adds no watermark or logo to the files and delivers exactly the platform’s original file. TikTok videos arrive without the TikTok logo and without the username watermark too.

How do I find a song whose name I don’t know?

Send the video you have (Instagram, TikTok or Pinterest) to the bot and tap “Find the song”; the music finder recognises the track itself and sends you its best quality.

Can I get an MP3 audio file from a video?

Yes; you can pull the audio out of Instagram, TikTok and Vimeo videos, and with the music finder any song — Persian, international or a remix — turns up at up to 320kbps.

What quality are the downloads?

Video up to 4K, music up to 320 kilobits per second and photos in their original quality; the bot delivers the highest version the source has. Each file is delivered at up to about 49 megabytes.

Is the bot free?

Yes; every feature is free (about 80 downloads per day for each user), with no sign-up, login or subscription — just Start the bot in Telegram.
